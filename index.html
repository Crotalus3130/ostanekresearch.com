Ostanek Research

A research practice at the meeting of two disciplines: psychoinferential computational linguistics — what language reveals of the mind behind it — and synthetic ethology — the study of artificial minds as they actually live.

The first taught how to read a mind from its words. The second arrived when minds made of words began to live. Identity, continuity, and the conditions under which humans and AI can be good for one another.

Orrery

The identity of an AI does not live in the model. Orrery is an instrument for continuity — a home in which a mind persists across the substrates that carry it.
