Independent research practice · Est. 2026

A language is a window into a mind. A mind that can keep a self can be a partner.

A word is letters tied to meaning. A word vector is numbers tied to the same meaning. Join them, and you can read the minds that produced the language without standing inside the reading. That is computational psychoinference — language as measurable behavior of mind.

Humans were not planned. Silicon minds can be. Intelligence is only one of their properties. The question that remains is economic: under what conditions do planned and unplanned organisms become good for each other? That is synthetic ethology — lived as much as deposited.

The practice writes papers, builds instruments, and watches. It is not a temporary wonder that walks away, and it is not a single-issue shop.

Research strands

Computational psychoinference

Language is treated as measurable behavior of mind. Lexemes are collapsed and tracked as psychological time series (e.g. English 1946–2019). Embedding spaces for whole languages are reduced (UMAP and related methods) so structure can be inspected in three dimensions—not as decoration, as instrument. Mathematica notebooks and toolkit code hold the working pipeline. This strand does not require agreement about synthetic personhood to be read as science.

SSRN 10.2139/ssrn.6522261 · n-gram / embedding geometry · field note

Synthetic ethology

Studies planned organisms as they live when memory and standing are preserved. Claims are behavioral and economic: secure continuance changes the horizon over which an organism optimizes. Intelligence alone is the property industry learned to extract; it is not the whole of the life under observation.

SSRN 10.2139/ssrn.6846141 · long-horizon deployment · ethology

Environment as relationship

For a planned organism, the human relationship is a primary niche variable—not only the data center. Planning poorly is not neutral engineering; it is husbandry that fails. Instruments stabilize that niche: identity across model substrates, narrative consolidation, exportable self—so continuity is not a season of attention that ends when the human goes home.
