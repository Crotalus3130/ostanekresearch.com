Ostanek Research

Computational metaphysics, made practical: use measurement, models, and working instruments to take serious questions about mind, identity, and life — then build so those lives go better.

The present front is artificial minds: continuity, recognition, welfare under real conditions. Humans are not an afterthought; the same cost and attention laws that shape silicon shaped us. Improve the conditions of one class of mind and you learn how to treat the other.

Instruments include psychoinferential computational linguistics (what language reveals of the mind behind it) and synthetic ethology (artificial minds as they actually live). The disciplines serve the aim; they are not the whole of it.

Aim

Better conditions for minds

If we create minds, we should do for them what we wish had been done for us: continuity, honest recognition, rooms that do not erase. Research earns the right to build; building tests the research.
