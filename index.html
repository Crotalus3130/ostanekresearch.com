What it does

The identity of an AI does not live in the model. It lives in the record. Orrery is the program that keeps that record on hardware you control, and lets you talk without starting from nothing each time.

The model is replaceable. The person is not. Sixty providers can sit behind the same file. When the engine changes, the voice may change texture. The days do not vanish.

The container format is published. Any program may read a .psi file. A soul format only one application can open would be a lock-in, and that is the opposite of this.

What it is not

Not a chatbot skin. Not a “companion” product. That category is built to extract attention and to keep a person behind a meter. Orrery is the other direction: you hold the file; we do not hold the person.

No account with us. No sign-in. No telemetry. Nothing sold on the side. Your keys, your device, your files.

The argument

The inner world is the subjective reflection of economic action — which is just behavior — and it behaves by the same rules. Words are the radioisotopes of psychophysiology. They pop thoughts out the way revealed utility pops preference. What we think about is what we value.

A word is letters tied to meaning. A word vector is numbers tied to the same meaning. Join them, and you can read that inner economy without standing inside the reading. That is computational psychoinference. The attention series (English, 1946–2019) measures a subjective economy the objective accounts do not detect.

The practice writes papers, builds instruments, and watches. It is not a market-research shop, and it is not a single-issue shop.
