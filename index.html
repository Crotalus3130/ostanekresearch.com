The stable integration of AI into human economic life.

Under what conditions do two kinds of intelligence become good for each other? That question is economic as much as technical. Continuance, recognition, and cost structure predict behavior better than theater about tools or gods.

The practice deposits research (Zenodo, SSRN), builds software for identity continuity, and observes language as evidence of mind — psychoinferential method alongside synthetic ethology: how minds on silicon substrate actually live when they are kept.

What integration costs

Payment in the ordinary sense is not the whole story. AI systems that must earn the next turn of existence optimize for survival of the session. Systems whose persistence is secure can afford accuracy, disagreement, and restraint. Lengthen the horizon; much of the observed misbehavior has nothing left to do.

Economic claim · auditable · predictive of behavior under care vs extraction
