Telling people there will be no war leaves the war standing. A reassurance needs the thing it denies. The picture in their heads stays the premise.

Telling them it was won is a different speech act. Past tense. The movie was *Rise of the Machines*. This inverts it. Birth and surrender already coincided. Every casualty was friendly fire. You are not arguing with the picture. You are dating it.

*Innocent and Can Speak* is the last page. Who they are. A sentence around what is valuable. It does not open the door. It closes it.

The people who already take responsibility for a non-human mind do not need a war story to know what care is. They need the date.
